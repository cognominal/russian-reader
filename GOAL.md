## Current state

Russian reader is designed for  people of level A2-B1 in russian
with translation in English. It uses articles from kremlin.ru.

The user learns by reading articles. An article appears in the 
left pane named article pane because 

A russian grammar glossary (russian GG)is included as an article.
It will help the learner to know about grammatical terms in
the learned language.

See [Glossary](./GLOSSARY.md) for the vocabulary used in the present
document. Unrelated to the grammar glossary.
